Chapter 03 · A tiny beginning
✨ 🎯 ✨

“You're a really good player!”

They played a few matches. Both got some great kills. He noticed her gameplay and decided to type a simple compliment instead of letting the moment pass.

You're a really good player! Want to play together again? 🎮
Sure! You're good too 😊
Then let's be friends?
Okayyy 💗

It was such a tiny conversation. Somehow, it opened a very big door.

Chapter 04 · Getting comfortable
🎙️ 🤍

A friendship before a voice

They started playing together more often. He tried to get her to talk on the mic, but she was not ready at first. And that was okay. They kept playing, teasing each other and slowly becoming familiar.

Sometimes friendship begins before you know someone's voice. You just begin to recognise their presence.

Little by little, the game stopped being only about winning. It became about who was there to play beside you.

Chapter 05 · Beyond the game
📸 💌

From lobby to Instagram

One day, he asked if she had Instagram. She shared her ID, and the conversations moved from the game into messages that could continue even after the match ended.

Do you use Instagram?
Yes, I do 😌
Send me your ID?
Here it is 💗

For a month or two, messages slowly built a friendship of its own. There were jokes, random conversations and little things to look forward to.

Chapter 06 · A new kind of closeness

“Your voice is so sweet.”

Maybe it was a simple compliment. But sometimes a voice makes a person feel more real, more familiar, and a little closer than before.

Chapter 08 · Finding our way back
🥺 💌 🌷

Even after a disagreement

Sometimes they got upset with each other. Sometimes she blocked him. He would try to make things right, and sometimes they found their way back to talking again.

The sweetest kind of closeness isn't about never disagreeing. It's about learning to listen, respect space, apologise sincerely and treat each other more gently.

The best way to care for someone is to let your actions make them feel safe, heard and respected.
